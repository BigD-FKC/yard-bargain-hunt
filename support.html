Get help

The app's own User Guide answers most questions: open the More tab, then User Guide. It's searchable, so you can type a word such as "Discover", "route" or "Kijiji".

Contact

For a problem, a question, or a suggestion, write to k12techman+yardbargainhunt@gmail.com. In the app, More → About this App → Contact Support starts the message with your app version and device, which helps answer it.

It helps to say what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead.

Common questions

  • Why is the map empty in my town? Few towns publish their garage-sale permits, so in most places the map fills as you add sales. Discover shows where sales are listed near you.
  • Does it work in Canada? Yes. Distances use kilometres if your device does, and Discover points to Canadian sites such as Kijiji.
  • Why can't the app read a Facebook link? Facebook needs you to sign in to see a post. Share a screenshot of the post instead.
  • Is it really free? Yes. No ads, no subscription and no in-app purchases.

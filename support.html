Get help

The app's own User Guide answers most questions: open the More tab, then User Guide. It's searchable, so you can type a word such as "New Sales", "route" or "Kijiji".

Contact

For a problem, a question, or a suggestion, write to k12techman+yardbargainhunt@gmail.com. In the app, More → About this App → Contact Support starts the message with your app version and device, which helps the developer answer it.

It helps to say what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead.

Common questions

  • Why is the map empty in my town? Few towns publish their garage-sale permits, so in most places the map fills as you add sales. In New Sales, tap Find Sales Near You to see where sales are listed near you. The map also shows shops to stop at on a sale day, such as flea markets and thrift stores.
  • Why did only a link come in? Some apps, such as Chrome, share only a page's link, not its words. The app doesn't open links to read them, because listing websites don't allow apps to copy their pages. Take a screenshot of the post and share the screenshot instead: it works in every app, and when the post is clear you can add it to a day's list right from the Share card. Or select the post's words and share those. Or keep the link and finish it later: open the draft in New Sales, tap Open Listing, copy the post's words, then come back and tap Paste. Safari shares a page's words along with its link.
  • Why do Start the Day and Next send only one sale to my map app? Plans change during the day. Yard Bargains keeps your plan and updates it each time you tap Next or Skip; your map app just gets you to the next sale. To see the whole route, tap Whole Route in Apple Maps.
  • Why did a sale I shared go to New Sales instead of my list? The Share card offers Add to List only when the kind of sale, the address and a day are all clear. When the app opens, it checks once more: a sale that looks like one you already have, or a day whose list is full or View Only, goes to New Sales instead, and the app says why.
  • What are spots? Places you mark to come back to, such as a sale sign you drove past: tap Mark This Spot on the map, or say "Mark a spot in Yard Bargains" to Siri. A spot isn't a sale. It stays on that iPhone only and clears itself away after 14 days.
  • How do I share a list? On the List, tap the Share button at the top right. Invite People shares the list itself with people who have Yard Bargains and iCloud, and changes show for everyone. A shared list runs the day Together, with one route and the same next sale for everyone, or Each on Their Own; choose under Run the Day in the same menu. Send a Copy sends one message with the sales that anyone can read, on any phone. The User Guide's Sharing a List tells you more.
  • Do I need a new iPhone? No. Every feature works on any iPhone or iPad that runs iOS 26. Newer models with Apple Intelligence get extra help reading posts, but every device reads them.
  • Where did To Check go? Since version 0.2 it's called New Sales. Every new sale comes in there, and Add to List puts it on the list for the day you'll go. The Route tab is now List, where you plan each day, and Start the Day opens the Route. Discover is now Find Sales.
  • Can it remind me about a sale? Yes. On a sale, tap Remind Me and choose a time, such as the evening before. Some posts give the address only the night before; write yourself a note, set a reminder, and tap Keep as Draft until you know.
  • I followed signs to a sale. How do I add it? On today's List, tap I'm at a Sale. The sale where you're standing goes on today's list as the sale you're at. To change its kind or address, touch and hold it, then tap Edit Sale.
  • The post gives directions, not an address. Can I add it? Yes. In Check Sale, type two streets that meet in Choose the Address, or tap Pick on the Map and move the map until the pin is where the sale starts. The app sends Apple Maps only the pin's place, to name the street near it.
  • How do I start over? In the app, tap More, then Settings, then Erase All Sales and Lists. It deletes every sale, draft and list and keeps your settings. It doesn't touch Flip It, which has its own Erase All Finds and Clean Slate, under Your Records.
  • What is Flip It? A record of what you buy at sales to resell: what you paid, where each find is, where it's listed, what it sold for, and your profit. Tap Flip It at the bottom of the screen; the Calendar is then in More, just below Free Trial and Purchases. To swap them, tap More, then Settings, then Third Tab.
  • Does Check an Item know what something is worth? No. It reads the item's words or barcode on your iPhone and opens eBay's sold prices and nearby asking prices in your web browser, or in eBay's or Facebook's own app if you have it, so you can see for yourself. eBay may ask you to sign in to show sold prices; a free account works. Pay at Most is worked out from the price you type and your profit goal. It's a guide, not an appraisal.
  • Can I get my Flip It records out? Yes. In Flip It, tap the More button, then Your Records, then Export. It makes spreadsheet files of your finds and other costs that open in Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets.
  • Where did my old sales go? Sales clear themselves away 30 days after their last day, and so do past days' lists. Nothing about them is kept, so the app has no history of where you went.
  • Does it work in Canada? Yes. Distances use kilometers if your device does. Find Sales lists Canadian sites such as Kijiji, and ventesdegarage.ca in Quebec.
  • Is it free? It's free to download, with a 14-day free trial of everything. After that, adding new sales, spots or finds needs a one-time purchase: Yard Bargains, Flip It, or both together for less. Everything you've saved stays yours either way, and you can still open, change and export it. No ads and no subscription.
  • I bought it on another iPhone or iPad. In the app, tap More, then Free Trial and Purchases, then Restore Purchases. Purchases go with your Apple Account.
  • How do I get a refund? Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are too: go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Get help

The app's own User Guide answers most questions: open the More tab, then User Guide. It's searchable, so you can type a word such as "New Sales", "route" or "Kijiji".

Contact

For a problem, a question, or a suggestion, write to k12techman+yardbargainhunt@gmail.com. In the app, More → About this App → Contact Support starts the message with your app version and device, which helps the developer answer it.

It helps to say what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead.

Common questions

  • Why is the map empty in my town? Few towns publish their garage-sale permits, so in most places the map fills as you add sales. In New Sales, tap Find Sales Near You to see where sales are listed near you. The map also shows shops to stop at on a sale day, such as flea markets and thrift stores.
  • Why did only a link come in? Some apps, such as Chrome, share only a page's link, not its words. The app doesn't open links to read them, because listing websites don't allow apps to copy their pages. Select the post's words and share those, or take a screenshot and add it with + in New Sales. For Facebook posts, a screenshot works best. Or keep the link and finish it later: open the draft in New Sales, tap Open Listing, copy the post's words, then come back and tap Paste. Safari shares a page's words along with its link.
  • Why do Start and Next send only one sale to my map app? Plans change during the day. Yard Bargains keeps your plan and updates it each time you tap Next or Skip; your map app just gets you to the next sale. To see the whole route, tap Whole Route in Apple Maps.
  • How do I share a list? On the List, tap the Share button at the top right. Invite People shares the list itself with people who have Yard Bargains and iCloud, and changes show for everyone. Send a Copy sends one message with the sales that anyone can read, on any phone. The User Guide's Sharing a List tells you more.
  • Do I need a new iPhone? No. Every feature works on any iPhone or iPad that runs iOS 26. Newer models with Apple Intelligence get extra help reading posts, but every device reads them.
  • Where did To Check go? Since version 0.2 it's called New Sales. Every new sale comes in there, and Add to List puts it on the list for the day you'll go. The Route tab is now List, and Discover is now Find Sales.
  • Can it remind me about a sale? Yes. On a sale, tap Remind Me and choose a time, such as the evening before. Some posts give the address only the night before; write yourself a note, set a reminder, and tap Keep as Draft until you know.
  • I followed signs to a sale. How do I add it? On today's List, tap I'm at a Sale. The sale where you're standing goes on today's list as the sale you're at. To change its kind or address, touch and hold it, then tap Edit Sale.
  • How do I start over? In the app, tap More, then Settings, then Erase All Sales and Lists. It deletes every sale, draft and list and keeps your settings.
  • Where did my old sales go? Sales clear themselves away 30 days after their last day, and so do past days' lists. Nothing about them is kept, so the app has no history of where you went.
  • Does it work in Canada? Yes. Distances use kilometers if your device does. Find Sales lists Canadian sites such as Kijiji, and ventesdegarage.ca in Quebec.
  • Is it really free? Yes. No ads, no subscription and no in-app purchases.

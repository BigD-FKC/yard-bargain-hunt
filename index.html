Yard Bargains

Yard sales, garage sales, estate sales, auctions, craft fairs and rummage sales near you, for iPhone and iPad.

Yard Bargains helps you find yard sales, garage sales, estate sales, in-person auctions, craft fairs and rummage sales near you, see them on a map, and plan the day's route around each sale's opening hours. It works in the United States and Canada.

Bring in the sales you find: take a screenshot of a post and share it to Yard Bargains, share the post's words, paste it, or photograph a sale sign, and the app reads it into a sale, linked to where it came from. When a shared post is clear, tap Add to List right there and it's on the list for the day you'll go; otherwise check it in New Sales first. Passing a sign during the week? Mark a spot, by tapping or by asking Siri, and come back to it later. Find Sales shows where sales are listed around any place, and the map shows the garage-sale permits some cities publish and shops to stop at, such as flea markets and thrift stores.

Plan your stops with Must-hit and Maybe, then let the Route take you from one sale to the next, and share a day's list with family who hunt with you, following one route together or each on their own. Or send a copy of a sale or a day's list to anyone, as a message they can read on any phone.

Buy to resell? Flip It keeps a record of your finds. At the sale table, Check an Item reads the item or its barcode and opens what similar ones sold for on eBay and what people nearby are asking, and shows the most to pay. Then track each find from listed to sold, with your selling places' fees, booth rent, your profit, a year-end summary and a spreadsheet export.

Try everything free for 14 days, then buy what you use, once: Yard Bargains, Flip It, or both. No subscription, no account to make, no ads, no tracking, and your data stays on your iPhone or iPad, and in your own iCloud if you're signed in to it.
